# Stock car pictures

The site shows a realistic picture of each car in **two angles**. These pictures are made free in the **Gemini app** (gemini.google.com) or **Google AI Studio** (aistudio.google.com), using the prompts below. They are AI-generated, and the site labels them that way.

## How to make one

1. Open gemini.google.com and start a new chat. If there's a "Create image" option, pick it.
2. Copy **one** prompt from below and paste it in.
3. If the car looks right (correct model, steering wheel on the right, plates blank), download the picture.
4. Save it in this `photos` folder with the **exact file name** shown, for example `golf-front.jpg`. PNG is fine too: `golf-front.png`.
5. Refresh the site. The picture appears when you switch the studio to **Front** or **Side**.

Tips:
- If the car is wrong (wrong generation, extra badges, left-hand drive), ask for a new one, or reply "Make it right-hand drive and keep everything else the same."
- Keep pictures **wide (16:9)** and roughly **1600–2400 pixels** across.
- If you hit the daily free limit, carry on the next day. Do the 3 pilot cars first: **Golf GTI, GR Supra, BMW M3**.

## The two styles

- **Front** (`<car>-front.jpg`): front three-quarter view on pale paving in front of a concrete wall, overcast daylight. Like the black BMW example.
- **Side** (`<car>-side.jpg`): exact side profile on a seamless grey studio backdrop, car facing left. Like the grey M4 example.

## Prompts

Each prompt is complete. Paste it as it is.

### Front template

> Photorealistic professional automotive photograph of a completely standard, factory-stock **{CAR}** in **{COLOUR}**, UK-specification right-hand drive. Front three-quarter view showing the front and the left side of the car, camera at headlight height, the whole car in frame with space around it. Parked on light-grey square paving slabs in front of a plain pale concrete wall, soft overcast daylight, gentle reflections on the paint. Standard factory wheels, ride height, badges and lights, no modifications. UK-style number plates with no letters or numbers on them. No people, no other cars, no added text or logos. Wide 16:9 image.

### Side template

> Photorealistic professional automotive studio photograph of a completely standard, factory-stock **{CAR}** in **{COLOUR}**, UK-specification right-hand drive. Exact side profile, perfectly side-on, the car facing left (front of the car on the left of the picture), camera at wheel-hub height, the whole car in frame with space around it. Seamless mid-grey studio backdrop and floor, soft even studio lighting, subtle reflection on the floor. Standard factory wheels, ride height, badges and lights, no modifications. UK-style number plates with no letters or numbers on them. No people, no added text or logos. Wide 16:9 image.

## Notes

- Google adds an invisible SynthID watermark to its AI images. Free app images may also carry a small visible mark.
- These are AI pictures, so small details (grille shape, badges) can be slightly off. The site labels them "AI-generated image".
- Don't use photos from Google Images or manufacturer websites: those belong to someone else.
