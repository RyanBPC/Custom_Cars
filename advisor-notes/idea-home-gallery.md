# Idea: home page with a gallery of car builds

From Ryan's ideas chat, 2026-11-04. Status: **ready to build. Do this BEFORE the AI 360 test (idea-ai-360-splat.md).**

## What Ryan wants
A main page that shows pictures of cars. Clicking a car opens the 360° 3D studio we already have, with that car loaded.

## Agreed design
- **Look:** dark and sporty but clean. Dark grey background, one bright accent colour, big photos.
- **Top bar:** logo, plus links to Home, Build Your Own and AI Photo.
- **Hero:** one wide photo of the best build, with a "Start building" button.
- **Grid of cards:** ready-made Golf GTI builds.
  - The first card is **"Build your own"**, which opens a stock Golf.
  - Other build examples: Track Golf, Stance Golf, Clean Daily, Show Car.
  - Each card shows a photo, a name and a few mod tags (e.g. "BBS wheels", "GT wing").
- **Clicking a card** opens the existing studio with that build's paint, wheels and aero already applied.

## Notes
- **Card photos:** use the site's own Photo Quality renders of each build. They're free and match exactly what opens. No paid AI.
- **Room for more cars:** build the card list so a new car model can be added later as another card, e.g. another car or an AI 360 car if that test works.
- **Keep it local:** no GitHub.
