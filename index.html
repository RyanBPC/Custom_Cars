      About

      A car configurator you can talk to

      What it is

      Custom Cars lets you plan modifications for popular UK cars, from a first car to a hot hatch, and see them fitted in 3D before you spend anything. Pick parts from the options, or describe what you want in plain English and the build advisor fits it for you.

      How it works

      1. Start from one of the models, or a stock car.
      2. Choose paint, wheels, ride height, aero and performance.
      3. Walk round it in 3D, sit inside, and turn it into an AI photo.

      Good to know

      Prices are estimates for parts and fitting at UK specialists. AI pictures are always labelled. Tell your insurer about every modification before you drive.
